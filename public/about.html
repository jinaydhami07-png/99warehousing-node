The Origin

Why We Built 99Warehousing

In 2019, co-founders Neeraj Agarwal and Priya Krishnamurthy were negotiating an 80,000 sq ft lease for Neeraj's FMCG distribution company in Bhiwandi. Despite months of search, they couldn't find a single reliable source of verified industrial listings with transparent per-sq-ft pricing.

Every broker quoted different rates for the same property. Floor plans were approximate. Structural certifications were photocopies of photocopies. The entire process was opaque, time-consuming, and expensive.

They built 99Warehousing to fix exactly that — starting with Mumbai, expanding to 48 cities, and today operating India's only industrial-first, verification-first real estate marketplace.

The 99Warehousing Verified Process

Every 99Warehousing Verified badge represents a rigorous 4-stage process that no generic property portal can match.

1
Document Review
RERA certificate, ownership proof, structural clearance, NOC — all reviewed by our legal team before the listing goes live.
2
On-Site Inspection
A 99Warehousing field officer visits every property to verify dimensions, clear height, loading docks, and condition against claimed specifications.
3
Rate Benchmarking
The listed rate is cross-checked against our proprietary database of 2,400+ recent transactions in the micro-market.
4
✦
99Warehousing Verified Badge
Awarded only after all three stages pass. The badge is renewed quarterly — so you always know the status is current.

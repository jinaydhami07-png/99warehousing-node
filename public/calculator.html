Frequently Asked Questions
How accurate is this calculator? +
This calculator uses 99Warehousing's proprietary dataset of 2,400+ verified transactions across 48 cities, updated quarterly. Rates are median market rates for each city-type combination. Actual rates may vary based on micro-location, lease term, and specific facility conditions.
What does the brokerage estimate include? +
Industry standard brokerage for industrial leases in India is typically 1 month's rent for transactions up to 5 years. For longer-term deals or BTS projects, the brokerage is separately negotiated. 99Warehousing brokers are transparent about their fees upfront.
Is GST applicable on industrial warehouse lease? +
Yes. GST at 18% applies on rent paid for commercial/industrial property. However, if you are registered under GST, you can claim input tax credit on this GST. The calculator shows the GST amount separately for your planning purposes.
What is included in the security deposit? +
Security deposits for industrial warehouses are typically 3 months' rent, though Grade A parks and premium properties may require up to 6 months. The deposit is fully refundable at lease end, subject to property condition.
